# [P2.3.2] Advanced appointment management, schedule conflict prevention, and owner record integration

## Why This Task Mattered

As clinic operations scale, administrators and pet owners require reliable scheduling, conflict prevention, fast search/filtering, and comprehensive patient & owner history. Prior to this task:
1. `getAvailableSlots` had a database query bug where open schedules for other dates leaked into slot generation, causing duplicate appointment cards during owner booking.
2. Admins could create overlapping schedule times on the same date without warning.
3. Managing schedules required manual date input rather than quick calendar interactions.
4. Appointments and Owner Directory lacked fast search, status filtering, pagination, and unified appointment history views.

## What Was Verified

- **Specific Date Slot Query Fix**: Updated `getAvailableSlots` in `src/services/appointments.ts` to strictly query `.eq("specific_date", specificDate)` instead of using a loose `.or` condition that leaked unclosed schedules from other dates.
- **Overlap Schedule Validation**: Added pre-submission validation in `src/app/dashboard/schedules/new/page.tsx` (`fStart < sEnd && fEnd > sStart`) to block creating or updating overlapping time slots on the same date with an immediate error toast.
- **Interactive Calendar Shortcuts**: Added `onDoubleClick` handlers on calendar day cells in `src/app/dashboard/schedules/page.tsx` to automatically navigate to `/dashboard/schedules/new?date=YYYY-MM-DD` and pre-fill the clicked date.
- **Appointments Tab Filtering & Pagination**: Added real-time search (pet, service, owner, reason), status filtering tabs (All, Requested, Confirmed, Completed, Cancelled), date picker filter, and page pagination (6 items per page) in `src/components/dashboard/tabs/appointments-tab.tsx`.
- **Owner Directory & Record Integration**:
  - `listOwnerRegistry` query in `src/services/pets.ts` updated to join owner appointments.
  - `src/components/dashboard/tabs/owners-tab.tsx` updated with owner initials avatar, pet & visit badges, embedded appointment preview card, search, and pagination.
  - `src/app/user/[ownerId]/page.tsx` upgraded with owner metrics (Pets, Total Visits, Active Visits) and a complete **Appointment History & Requests** list.
- **Appointment Detail Page 2-Tab View**:
  - `src/app/dashboard/appointments/[id]/page.tsx` and `src/components/dashboard/appointments/appointment-detail-view.tsx` transformed into a modern layout with Owner & Schedule header cards.
  - Implemented 2 distinct tabs:
    1. **Pets Details Tab**: Patient pet card (Species, Breed, Sex, DOB, Age, Color, Notes) with direct link to medical records, plus other linked pets.
    2. **Appointment History Tab**: Complete list of owner/pet appointments with status badges, dates/times, reasons, timeline milestones (*Requested, Confirmed, Completed, Cancelled*), and a "Currently Viewing" highlight tag.
