# [P5.2] Deliver owner portal for linked pets, appointments, permitted records, invoices, and receipts

## Why This Task Mattered
Pet owners need a dedicated, intuitive, and secure portal to track their registered pets, view upcoming veterinary visits, access permitted clinical and immunization records, and review itemized invoices and official payment receipts with popup-blocker-safe printing. Strict role boundaries and Row Level Security (RLS) ensure pet owners can only access their own linked pets, appointments, and billing data without leaking sensitive records from other clients.
