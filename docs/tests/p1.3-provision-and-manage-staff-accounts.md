# [P1.3] Provision and manage administrator and veterinarian accounts

## Why This Task Mattered

Clinic administrators need to grant, change, and remove staff access without exposing privileged credentials. This task also ensures a deactivated account cannot continue into the dashboard and that the clinic always retains an active administrator.

## What Was Verified

- `npm run lint` and `npx tsc --noEmit`: passed; lint reported only pre-existing warnings.
- Human acceptance checklist: passed in the local application with real staff-account and email delivery checks.

## Human Evidence

- [x] Signed in as an administrator and opened `/dashboard/team` → staff management loaded and other dashboard pages continued to work.
- [x] Invited a veterinarian with a real email and full name → the action showed a pending state, a success toast appeared, and Supabase delivered the invite email.
- [x] Checked the invited account in the staff list → it showed Veterinarian and Active, with no service-role credential exposed in browser tools or page source.
- [x] Changed a staff role and refreshed → the success toast appeared and the new value persisted.
- [x] Deactivated the veterinarian → a success toast appeared, the row changed to Deactivated, and the account was refused at login. A screenshot showed Supabase's `user_banned` result.
- [x] Reactivated the veterinarian → a success toast appeared and the account regained dashboard access for its role.
- [x] Attempted to deactivate or demote the final active administrator → the action was blocked with a clear error and an active administrator remained.
- [x] Signed in as a veterinarian and owner, then opened `/dashboard/team` → access was denied or redirected; team administration remained admin-only.

## Result

Pass. Staff lifecycle actions, final-admin protection, and role-based access boundaries were verified by a human and are safe to close.
